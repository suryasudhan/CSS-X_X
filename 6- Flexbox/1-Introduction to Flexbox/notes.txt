                    WHAT IS FLEXBOX : (it MOVES in LEFT TO RIGHT]

                @> FLEXBOX is a one-dimensional layout mobel making it simple to arrange items in rows or columns and distribute space

                        flex-direction : column;

                                    1
                                    2
                                    3

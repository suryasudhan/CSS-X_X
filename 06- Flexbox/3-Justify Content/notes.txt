                            JUSTIFY CONTENT & SHIFTING MAIN AXIS  

                  
                    SPACING ON THE SHIFTED MAIN AXIS(there are 2 types)

                              * General block container
                        justify-content:center;
                                > differnt values will have no effect in placement of FLEX items as no space

                              * Container with a set height
                        justify-content:center;
                                > differnt values will have an effect in placement of FLEX items as space

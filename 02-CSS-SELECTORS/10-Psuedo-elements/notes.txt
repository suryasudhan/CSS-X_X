                         #text-styling
              *used to enhance visual apperance to text*

              * : : first-letter(drop cap)
              * : : first-line(paragraph opening line)  

              ex:

                p::first-letter{
                                  text-transform: uppercase;
                                  font-size: 30px;
                                  font-weight: 700;
                                }


                  p::first-line{
                                text-transform: uppercase;
                                font-size: 30px;
                                font-weight: 800;
                              }

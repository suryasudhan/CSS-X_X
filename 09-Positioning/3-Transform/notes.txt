                                  TRANSFORM PROPERTY

             @> Applies visual TRANSFORMATIONs to an ELEMENTS like scaling , rotating , TRANSLATE and (skewing-applies a shear transformation (also known as a transvection) to distort an element on the 2D plane by tilting it along the x-axis and/or y-axis]                        

                 [transform : scale(2,0.5); -> transformation function}

                 VALUES : scale() , skew() , rotate() , translate()

                          ====ADDED IMG-TRANSFORM PROPERTY====

>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>

                             TRANSFORMATION FUNCTIONS

    1 .  SCALE : resizes element HORIZONTALLY & VERTICALLY

        (transform : scale(2,0.5); -> x-axis scale factor | y-axis scale factor}                       


    2 .  SKEW : Distort element by slanting it HORIZONTALLY & VERTICALLY

        (transform : skew(20deg,50deg); -> x-axis slant angle | y-angle slant angle}


    3 .  ROTATE : rotates element around its center

        (transform : rotate(45deg); -> angle}     
  

    4 .  TRANSLATE : moves element HORIZONTALLY & VERTICALLY from its position

         (transform : translate(50% , -200px); -> x-axis transformation | y-axis transformation}
